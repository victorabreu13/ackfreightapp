What the platform does

The platform lets our administrators track trip requests, assign drivers, log completed trips, and generate invoices for completed shipments. Invoicing is done through an integration with QuickBooks Online: when an administrator sends an invoice, the platform creates and emails that invoice using the QuickBooks Online API, on behalf of ACK Freight.
