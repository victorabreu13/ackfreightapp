Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy describes what information ACK Freight's dispatch and billing platform collects, how it's used, and how it relates to our QuickBooks Online integration.

Information we collect

  • Account information for administrators, drivers, and customers: name, email address, and role.
  • Trip and shipment details: pickup/delivery locations, dates, AWB (air waybill) numbers, piece counts, and weights.
  • Driver trip logs: time worked, proof-of-delivery photos or documents, and assigned driver.
  • Billing information administrators configure: a customer's invoicing rate, used to calculate invoice amounts.

We do not collect or store payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or other payment credentials. The platform does not process payments.

How we use this information

Trip and billing data is used to coordinate shipments, log completed trips, and — when an administrator chooses to invoice a completed shipment — to create and send an invoice through the QuickBooks Online API. That invoice includes the customer's name, email, invoice line description (route, date, and AWB number), and the invoice total. QuickBooks Online then emails the invoice to the customer directly.

Where data is stored

Platform data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) services, including Firestore (database) and Cloud Storage (uploaded documents/photos). Invoice records created through the integration are also stored in the connected QuickBooks Online company, subject to Intuit's own privacy practices.

Who we share data with

We share the minimum data necessary with QuickBooks Online (Intuit) to create and send invoices, as described above. We do not sell or share platform data with any other third party.

Data retention

We retain trip, billing, and account data for as long as needed to operate the platform and to keep accurate business records, and delete or anonymize it when it's no longer needed for those purposes.
